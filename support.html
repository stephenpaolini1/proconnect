Support Services on Camp Day

Every ProConnect camp runs with the same core services in place. Because each camp sets its own schedule, service hours are listed relative to that camp's sessions rather than fixed clock times.

  1. Equipment & Field Access
    • Cages and field open 30 minutes before each session.
    • Bullpen available on request during sessions.
  2. Registration & Check-In
    • Check-in opens 60 minutes before the first session.
    • Late check-in accepted through the morning session.
  3. Parking & Facility Access
    • Lot opens 30 minutes before check-in.
    • Facility closes 30 minutes after the final session.
  4. Food & Local Options
    • On-site concessions run during session breaks.
    • Each camp listing links nearby dining and lodging for traveling families. You can also search for dining near your camp or find nearby lodging to plan the day.
