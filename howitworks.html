How ProConnect Works

ProConnect brings three groups together in one place: players who want to run camps, facilities with open space to fill, and families looking for quality coaching. Here is how a camp goes from an idea to opening day.

From Idea to Camp Day in Three Steps

  1. A player lists a camp. An experienced player creates a listing with the dates, skills, and age groups they want to coach, then chooses an available facility to host it.
  2. The facility is booked. The facility confirms the request and reserves the space. Every booking, date, and roster is managed from one shared calendar.
  3. Families register. The camp goes live in the Find a Camp listings, where families can browse by location, read the details, and register their athlete online.
