Why Run a Camp With ProConnect

You already know the game. ProConnect handles the logistics so you can focus on coaching. List your camp, book a facility, and reach families all in one place.

  • Run offseason camps without chasing down facilities yourself.
  • Set your own dates, skills, age groups, and pricing.
  • Reach families already searching for quality coaching.
  • Manage every booking and roster from one shared calendar.

How to List a Camp

  1. Create your player profile with your background, the skills you coach, and the age groups you want to work with.
  2. Choose an available facility, set your dates and pricing, and confirm the space for your camp.
  3. Promote your camp through the Find a Camp listings and start receiving registrations from families.
