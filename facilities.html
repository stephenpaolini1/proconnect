Why Host Camps With ProConnect

Your facility already has the space. ProConnect fills it with experienced players who bring their own following and connects you with the families looking to book.

  • Fill off-peak field, cage, and court time with camps that book.
  • Work with vetted players who have real playing experience.
  • Manage every booking, date, and roster from one calendar.
  • Set your own pricing and availability. You control your space.

How to List Your Facility

  1. Create your facility profile with photos, location, and the spaces you have available.
  2. Set your pricing, available dates, and the types of camps you want to host.
  3. Start receiving booking requests from players and confirm the ones that fit your schedule.
